What Ghanaians think of the president's performance

The latest polls disagree: Global InfoAnalytics put President Mahama's job approval at 72% in July 2026, the IEA at 58.9% in May 2026. TARIN lists every published national poll on the president's performance it can trace, with who ran it, when, how many people were asked and where each figure was read.

Why polls differ

Same weeks, different answers

In December 2025 Global InfoAnalytics and the IEA were 1 point apart (67% and 68%). In May–June 2026 they were 12.1 points apart: the IEA found 58.9% (May 2026), Global InfoAnalytics 71% (30 May – 12 Jun 2026).

The pollsters measure in different ways. Global InfoAnalytics' Mussa Dankwah has put the gap down to timing, sample size and weighting: his firm interviewed more than 1,500 people in the Ashanti Region alone, against about 1,000 nationwide for the IEA, and weights its sample by registered voters (Rainbow Radio). In the IEA's May survey, people who disapproved most often named the economy (30.9%) and electricity supply (29.9%), during the power cuts of May 2026 (Graphic Online).

TARIN can't say which is nearer the truth, so it shows every poll as published, never an average of pollsters that ask different people in different ways.

Method

How the polls are collected, and what this page leaves out

Where the figures come from. 2 of the 9 polls are read from the pollster's own report; for the other 7, the pollster's report wasn't found online, so the figures come from news reports of the release, linked in the table. A figure the source doesn't give is left blank, never filled in.

What counts. National polls of adults or voters in Ghana that ask about the president's job performance and say who ran them and when the results came out. Party-run polls, online straw polls and anything read from social media are left out.

The checks. Every poll goes through five rules before it is shown:

    Downloads. polls-approval.csv (every poll, one row each) and polls.json (everything on this page, with each source link).
