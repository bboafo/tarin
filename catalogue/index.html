What's in the store, and how fresh it is

Current: GSS updated the table in the last four months. Lagging: in the last year. Frozen: not since. Frozen tables are still worth having: their history is complete to the month shown, and nothing newer exists on StatsBank. The Bank of Ghana's exchange rates are read every day and are current while they reach the past week.
