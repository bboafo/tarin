    Sources

    Where the numbers come from, and how fresh they are

    SourceCoversStatusLatest fileSHA-256

    The two CPI sources overlap from 2021 and are compared month by month. Where they agree, figures come from StatsBank up to January 2026 and from the annex after it. The other StatsBank tables are listed, with their dates, in the catalogue. The Bank of Ghana's rates are read twice a day; their monthly averages are compared with StatsBank's exchange-rate table. The World Bank's workbook is read at the start of each month; its Brent price is compared with the IMF's. World diesel prices come from the US Energy Information Administration through FRED and are read once a day.
